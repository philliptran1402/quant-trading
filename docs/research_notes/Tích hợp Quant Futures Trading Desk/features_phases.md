## Chiến lược nào có thể chuyển và chức năng research cần có?

### Takeaway

Nên chuyển một chiến lược giá đơn giản thành module thuần, có version và testable, thay vì đưa nguyên script vào service. MACD hoặc Dual Thrust là ứng viên demo đầu tiên; RSI/Parabolic SAR cũng có thể làm sau khi định nghĩa rõ execution timing. Khả năng chuyển code chỉ nói về độ phù hợp kỹ thuật, không nói về performance.

### Inferences

- **Ứng viên theo thứ tự:** (1) MACD trend following cho MVP vì đơn giản, dễ xác định lookback và trạng thái long/flat; (2) Dual Thrust/breakout để chứng minh OHLC event/timestamp; (3) RSI hoặc Parabolic SAR sau khi thống nhất long/short, stop/exit và intrabar execution. Candidate selection là quyết định về phạm vi kỹ thuật, không phải nhận định alpha.
- **Registry thay vì một service mỗi script:** mỗi strategy là module versioned với metadata: `strategyId`, semantic version, mô tả, timeframe, warmup bars, schema/tham số hợp lệ, side support, signal timing, yêu cầu dữ liệu, cost support, default assumptions và code/image digest. Không cho user gửi Python tùy ý trong MVP.
- **Research workflow người dùng:** chọn venue/contract type/symbol/timeframe/date range → chọn strategy/version → cấu hình params và execution assumptions → xem preview data coverage → submit run → xem summary/curve/trade ledger/diagnostics → lưu snapshot → compare các run cùng dataset/assumptions → export markdown/CSV/JSON report. Parameter sweep nên được hỗ trợ có giới hạn và ghi lại số tổ hợp đã thử; không hiển thị “best params” như bằng chứng tổng quát.
- **Run reproducibility:** mỗi run ghi `runId`, caller/workspace, strategy ID/version + image/code digest, canonical params, engine version, deterministic seed nếu có random sampling, data source/venue/symbol/contract/interval/time range, data snapshot hash/version, candle count/gaps, cost model, initial equity, leverage/margin assumptions, created time, result hash và warnings. Lưu immutable run spec/result; điều này cho phép replay/audit mà không phải tin UI state.
- **Metrics phải có glossary:** net return, annualization basis, max drawdown (peak-to-trough), realized/unrealized PnL, trade count and definition, win rate, profit factor, average trade, exposure/time-in-market, turnover, fee, funding, slippage estimates, liquidation/maintenance events (chỉ khi engine hỗ trợ), benchmark comparison. Sharpe cần nêu periodicity, risk-free assumption và sample adequacy; không gọi Sharpe/Daily close là đánh giá an toàn.
- **Backtest validation levels:** minimum historical check (OHLCV integrity, duplicate/gap/out-of-order, timezone, incomplete last candle); signal check against hand-computable fixtures; no-lookahead timing; next-bar execution convention; fee/funding sensitivity; chronological train/validation/untouched test; walk-forward; compare simple benchmark; stress scenarios. Nếu sweep nhiều config, lưu trial count và dùng selection-bias diagnostics thích hợp (PBO/CSCV hoặc Reality Check ở nghiên cứu nâng cao); các phương pháp này giảm/đo một phần selection bias, không chứng minh tương lai.
- **Futures-specific simulation:** at minimum fees and funding cashflows with timestamps; specify if signal candles are contract-price, mark-price or index-price. Mark price history is a separate documented market-data endpoint, and official API exposes funding timestamps/rates. A bar-close OHLC strategy cannot faithfully model intrabar liquidation or order-book fill; label those limitations until mark-price path, margin rules, and fill model are implemented. — [Binance USDⓈ-M Market Data API](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data)
- **Data selection / survivorship:** use a point-in-time contract universe (including delisted/expired instruments where history exists) or explicitly constrain claims to a fixed selected symbol. Selecting today's liquid symbols and testing them historically creates survivorship/universe-selection risk. Track contract listing start/end, status and symbol filter snapshots. The specific backfill availability for delisted symbols needs provider verification (gap listed below).
- **Parameter management:** distinguish economic parameters (lookback/threshold), simulator assumptions (fee/slippage/fill), and risk sizing. Keep candidate ranges and sweep budgets explicit; prohibit editing a strategy after seeing final holdout without opening a new experiment/version. Keep a human-reviewed experiment log of rejected trials, not just winner.
- **Current Trading Desk baseline to evolve:** current Quant Lab is `app/src/components/panels/AdvancedWorkspace.tsx`; it calls `POST /api/v1/quant/backtests`, lets user pick a symbol/strategy/lookback and `Risk / trade %`, and renders a backtest state. Nest implementation is in `api/src/advanced-tools/advanced-tools.controller.ts` and `.service.ts`; it downloads Binance daily klines, has four hard-coded strategy labels, creates synthetic sample candles if Binance fails, and reports approximate metrics. The UI option “Funding neutral” does not fetch historical funding, so the label cannot imply funding-neutral PnL. — [Trading Desk Quant Lab component](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/app/src/components/panels/AdvancedWorkspace.tsx); [AdvancedToolsService](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/api/src/advanced-tools/advanced-tools.service.ts)
- Existing Desk also has market-data futures endpoints and paper orders/positions, so Quant should complement those systems: API owns session/workspace auth and data coordination; research engine owns deterministic signal/backtest calculation; paper workflow later routes user-authorized signals to existing paper API. — [Trading Desk market data controller](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/api/src/market-data/market-data.controller.ts); [paper trading module](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/api/src/paper-trading/paper-trading.controller.ts)

### Gaps

- Exact availability/retention for historical mark-price klines, old/delisted USDⓈ-M contracts, and funding records for every symbol must be checked against the chosen production provider before product requirements promise coverage.
- A realistic fill/slippage model requires a decision on data granularity and whether to buy/store trades or order-book data; OHLC bars alone do not determine queue position or market impact.
- The Desk's existing Quant Lab is a prototype and has no persisted experiment history in the inspected route; schema review is needed before choosing whether run persistence belongs in Desk Prisma or a separate research store.

## User workflow, service boundary, and output contract

### Takeaway

Keep the browser and user identity at Trading Desk; use the existing API as the authenticated orchestration boundary and a private Python research service as a deterministic calculation boundary. Begin synchronously/stateless for one backtest if its latency is acceptable; add persistent jobs, queue and run-history storage only when measured work size/concurrency calls for them.

### Inferences

#### Proposed product workflow

1. **Research / Strategy catalog:** browse a small approved set of strategies with plain-language rule, side mode, bar-close execution convention, supported instruments/timeframes, warm-up and limitations. Duplicate into an experiment draft; do not edit a published version in place.
2. **Configure data and assumptions:** select Binance USDⓈ-M perpetual (initial venue), contract/symbol, timeframe, UTC from/to, initial equity, sizing method, leverage cap if simulated, maker/taker fee tier, slippage model, funding data source and benchmark. Render data coverage/gaps and mark missing components before allowing run. Every setting has a definition tooltip and unit.
3. **Backtest:** submit immutable config; show queued/running/completed/failed/cancelled status; never convert an exchange/API error to invented historical prices. A demo fixture may exist only in a visibly isolated demo mode and cannot share the “historical backtest” label.
4. **Read results:** show net/gross returns, drawdown, trade count, exposure, turnover, fees/funding/slippage, benchmark, equity and drawdown curves, trade ledger (signal time, assumed entry/exit time/price, quantity, fees, funding, realized PnL), coverage/warnings and exact assumptions. Mark each value `measured`, `estimated`, `excluded`, or `unavailable` when that distinction matters.
5. **Compare runs:** compare only when instrument, interval, date range, initial equity and cost semantics match, or show differences prominently. Compare against buy/hold or flat benchmark; compare OOS and walk-forward slices separately; show parameter/trial count and avoid sorting a giant sweep by raw Sharpe without selection-bias context.
6. **Report/export:** save immutable run spec/result under caller/workspace access controls; export run JSON/CSV and a human-readable Markdown/PDF later. Include strategy version/code digest, data provenance/hash, engine version, cost assumptions, validation split and limitation notice in every report.
7. **Paper forward testing (later):** create a paper session from a pinned strategy version/config; stream or poll completed bars, produce timestamped signals; require explicit user start/stop; route proposals to the existing paper API only after server-side risk checks. Show feed freshness, missed bars, signal-to-paper-order/fill reconciliation, simulated fees/funding, PnL and kill state. A research run is never auto-promoted to paper or live.

#### Ownership boundary recommendation

- **Trading Desk API:** session auth, role/workspace authorization, request validation, venue/instrument allowlist, data collection/provenance, run access checks, rate/compute quotas, durable run lifecycle, UI-facing contract, paper authorization and audit ownership.
- **Quant Research Service:** no browser session, exchange API key or order interface. Receives bounded, schema-validated bars + strategy/version + assumptions; validates gap/order/finite values; computes deterministic results and returns structured diagnostics. Pin image/code digest. Restrict network egress where feasible; do not let request select arbitrary URL or import arbitrary module.
- **Transport:** private HTTP with service identity and request ID is a simple synchronous MVP. Keep payload bounded. For long parameter sweeps/walk-forward jobs, shift to async job API and queue with cancel/timeout/resource quotas; keep the same versioned request/result schema. NATS can be considered if it matches Desk operational standards, but should not be required for one short calculation.
- **Data boundary:** for initial Binance-only proof, Desk API can reuse its current market-data service and pass the resulting canonical bars to Python. If multi-venue/backfill becomes a requirement, put normalized historical candles/funding/mark data behind a dedicated data service or Gateway contract rather than allowing each strategy to fetch a venue directly.
- **Storage boundary:** persist run manifests/results in Desk DB for current user and history UI; persist content-addressed snapshots in object storage only when retention/size justifies it. A stateless calculation service avoids duplicate user/workspace identity and reduces operational state. Never key private run queries only by a caller-supplied ID.

#### API/UI response fields to plan

- Request: `schemaVersion`, `requestId`, `venue`, `marketType`, `symbol`, `interval`, `from`, `to`, `strategyId`, `strategyVersion`, `parameters`, `sizingModel`, `initialEquity`, `leverage/margin assumptions`, `feeModel`, `slippageModel`, `fundingMode`, `benchmark`, data source/snapshot reference, validation split and sweep budget.
- Result: `runId`, `status`, `createdAt`, `completedAt`, `requestHash`, `strategyDigest`, `engineVersion`, `datasetHash`, observed time coverage, source labels, candle count, gaps/duplicates, metrics glossary/version, curve, trade ledger, costs by category, warnings, execution model limits and result hash.
- Use decimal strings for raw monetary values/prices/quantities across service boundary; specify rounding for UI metrics. Reject NaN/Infinity, unknown strategy versions, oversized arrays, unsupported interval or invalid date range. Do not accept arbitrary symbol as sufficient validation: validate against current/historical instrument metadata and preserve metadata snapshot.
- `riskPercent` is currently ambiguous in Desk. Its prototype computes `allocation = equity * riskPercent / 100`, then multiplies price return by that allocation. That is exposure/allocation scaling, not risk-at-stop sizing and does not model a stop, margin or liquidation. Rename to `exposurePercent` for that meaning or define risk budget by stop-distance/contract unit before showing a “risk per trade” control. — [AdvancedToolsService](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/api/src/advanced-tools/advanced-tools.service.ts)

#### Operations / telemetry / audit

- Structured logs and traces: request/run ID, actor/workspace ID (avoid secrets), strategy/version/digest, dataset hash, queue wait, fetch duration, compute duration, bars processed, sweep count, status/error class, result size, timeout/cancel and worker memory/CPU. Do not log credential/session values or full proprietary datasets.
- Metrics: request rate, failure rate by dependency/reason, p50/p95 latency, active/queued jobs, bars/sec, timeout/cancel count, cache hit rate, data gap/error count, service restarts and paper-feed freshness/divergence. Alert on repeated data-source errors, stale feed and stuck jobs.
- Audit events: created run, changed draft, started/cancelled paper session, approved/denied paper action, changed limits, killed session; append actor, timestamp, before/after config hash, reason and correlation ID. Preserve immutable backtest manifest/result; corrections create a new run/version.
- Security controls: workspace/object-level authorization on every saved run and paper session; service-to-service identity; quotas/limits on period, bars, sweep combinations, concurrent jobs and response size; request deadlines; deny untrusted URL/code execution; safe error messages; dependency/image scanning and signed/pinned artifacts before production.
- Paper-run kill controls: stop producing new signals, cancel pending paper intents, flatten only if the existing paper model supports this operation and the policy explicitly says so; always preserve audit. Define behavior separately for feed outage, stale bars, engine crash, order API timeout, duplicate events, daily loss/exposure limit, and manual kill. A disabled signal engine and a flat account are different states.

### Gaps

- Need an owner decision on whether saved runs are per-user, shared workspace, or project-scoped; current desk checkout has evolving workspace/account semantics, so final schema should follow the current authorization model.
- Need a product choice on synchronous latency SLO, max bars/sweep size, retention and report formats before deciding if queue/object storage is necessary.
- Need verify current authentication/authorization guard wiring for all Advanced Tools endpoints and global rate limits in full Nest app; route annotations alone are not proof of enforcement.

## Phased roadmap, acceptance gates, and permission boundary

### Takeaway

Build in independently reviewable phases: define contract and one strategy; produce deterministic historical results with truthful data provenance; add futures cost/margin fidelity; run paper forward tests through existing Desk controls; then treat live execution as a separate product and risk authorization. Each phase should stop when its acceptance evidence fails, not advance because a headline metric looks attractive.

### Cited Findings

- Current Desk Quant endpoint downloads one-day Binance klines and falls back to generated sample candles if any fetch path fails; its current strategy set is four labels, but no actual historical funding series is used. This is an existing functional risk and shows why provenance and hard failures are phase-one requirements. — [AdvancedToolsService](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/api/src/advanced-tools/advanced-tools.service.ts); [Quant Lab UI](/Users/phitran/fublab/trading-desk/app/src/components/panels/AdvancedWorkspace.tsx)

#### Shared acceptance details

- **Data gate:** strict monotonic UTC open times; duplicate and gap detection; completed candles only; no forward-fill across market outage without an explicit rule; capture source/API version/query window/collection time and instrument metadata. Set a maximum tolerated missingness per interval; if exceeded, reject or mark result invalid.
- **Execution convention:** define signal computed after bar close and earliest possible next-bar fill; model gap-through stops and ambiguous OHLC path conservatively or use higher-resolution data. Do not assume close price fills an order placed after close.
- **Cost gate:** show both gross and net; fee on each entry/exit and partial fill; funding only at eligible funding timestamps while position held; slippage/spread assumptions independently toggled; sensitivity to higher costs. Existing constant `0.0004` fee cannot stand in for venue/account's actual fee tiers without an explicit assumption.
- **Sizing gate:** expose distinct choices: fixed notional/exposure, fixed quantity, or risk-budget-per-stop. State whether leverage multiplies notional, margin or loss budget; show position notional and required simulated margin. Do not label exposure percentage as “risk per trade”.
- **Anti-overfit gate:** predeclare holdout and benchmark before sweeping; record all configs/trials; optimize only in training window; validate with chronological split/walk-forward; preserve final untouched holdout; if user changes the strategy after viewing holdout, start a new experiment and mark the holdout consumed. PBO/Reality Check are later diagnostics if experiment counts/data support meaningful estimation, not a guarantee.
- **Run management:** versioned states `QUEUED/RUNNING/SUCCEEDED/FAILED/CANCELLED/INVALID_DATA`; idempotency key; timeout; retry only deterministic infrastructure failure; result snapshot immutable; new assumptions = new run. Avoid rerunning an exchange fetch under the same manifest if contents might change; persist or content-hash the exact input bars.
- **Telemetry:** log durations, statuses and IDs; measure feed freshness, API/service errors, queue depth, timeout, cancellation, bars/sec, memory, costs and PnL reconciliation. Alert separately for data quality, service health and strategy performance; telemetry should not write trading commands.
- **Permission boundary:** Phase 0–3 are research only; Phase 4 permits user-authorized paper simulation only; no exchange trading secret is available to the research container. Phase 5 requires an explicit separate decision and review. A kill switch for research stops jobs; for paper it stops new intents and applies documented paper-order policy; for live it must be controlled by OMS/risk/exchange adapter and tested as a separate safety property.

### Gaps

- No evidence-based universal paper-testing duration, trade count, drawdown gate, Sharpe threshold or acceptable slippage level exists for this project from the sources reviewed; product owner and research reviewer must set these before Phase 4.
- Exact order/position reconciliation and kill-switch behavior depends on existing Desk paper API semantics and future OMS/Gateway deployment configuration; inspect the live composition and persistence model before implementation.
- Further research is needed on exchange fee schedules/account tiers, liquidation fee/maintenance brackets, historical instrument delisting coverage, acceptable dataset retention, applicable policies, and which data provider terms allow storage/replay.
